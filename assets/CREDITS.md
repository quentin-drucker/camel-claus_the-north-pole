Our own: music (`assets/audio/music/`, an original chiptune arrangement of the public-domain melody
"Jingle Bells", J. L. Pierpont 1857) and jingles (`jingle_*.wav`, `sfx_jingle_pulse.wav`) are generated by
`art_src/tools/make_audio.py`. All 3D models and textures are generated by `art_src/tools/pixel_bake.py`.

`art_src/tools/build_ui_theme.gd`.
